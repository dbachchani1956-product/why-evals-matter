# Expense Policy 03 — Receipts and Submission

**Document ID:** expense-03-receipts
**Effective:** 1 January 2026

## When a receipt is required

An itemised receipt is required for any single expense above **EUR 25** or local
equivalent. Card statements are not accepted as a substitute for an itemised
receipt, because they do not show what was purchased.

Expenses of EUR 25 or below may be claimed without a receipt, up to a combined
total of EUR 100 per report.

## Submission deadline

Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the date the cost was
incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval and
may be rejected.

For multi-day trips the deadline runs from the final day of travel, not from each
individual expense date.

## Lost receipts

Where a receipt has been lost, the employee may submit a lost receipt declaration
stating the amount, date, vendor, and business purpose. A maximum of **two lost
receipt declarations per quarter** are permitted per employee.

## Foreign currency

Convert foreign currency at the rate published on the date of the transaction.
Where a card provider has already converted the amount, claim the converted
figure shown on the statement and attach it alongside the itemised receipt.

Conversion fees charged by the card provider are reimbursable.

## Non-reimbursable regardless of receipt

Alcohol beyond a working meal, personal entertainment, fines and penalties,
personal travel upgrades, and spouse or family travel costs are never
reimbursable, whether or not a receipt is provided.
