# Travel Policy 02 — Per Diem Allowances

**Document ID:** travel-02-perdiem
**Effective:** 1 January 2026

## What per diem covers

The daily per diem allowance covers meals, non-alcoholic drinks, and incidental
costs such as local public transport under €15 per journey, laundry on trips of
five nights or more, and tips.

Per diem does **not** cover hotel accommodation. Hotels are claimed separately
against the nightly caps in the expense limits policy.

## Daily rates by destination country

| Country | Daily rate |
| --- | --- |
| Germany | EUR 75 |
| France | EUR 80 |
| United Kingdom | GBP 70 |
| India | INR 3000 |
| United States | USD 95 |
| Netherlands | EUR 78 |
| Singapore | SGD 110 |

Rates are set annually by Finance and apply to the country where the employee
spends the night.

## Travel days

Days of departure and return are reimbursed at **50% of the daily rate**, because
at least one meal on each of those days is assumed to be taken at home or in
transit.

A trip of four calendar days consisting of two travel days and two full days is
therefore claimed as two days at full rate plus two days at half rate.

## Provided meals

Where a meal is provided — by a conference, a client, or included in the hotel
rate — the per diem for that day is reduced by 20% per provided meal.

## Claiming

Per diem is claimed as a single line per trip. Individual meal receipts are not
required for per diem claims.
