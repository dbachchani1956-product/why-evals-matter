# Travel Policy 01 — Air Travel

**Document ID:** travel-01-airfare
**Effective:** 1 January 2026

## Cabin class entitlement

| Flight duration | Permitted cabin | Approval needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 6 hours | Economy | None |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy | Line manager |
| 10 hours or more | Business | Line manager, Band 4 and above only |

Band 1 to Band 3 employees are not eligible for business class on any route,
regardless of flight duration.

## Booking window

Flights must be booked at least 14 calendar days before departure. Bookings made
inside 14 days require written justification from the line manager and are
reviewed by Finance before reimbursement.

## Airport lounge access

Airport lounge access, day passes, and lounge memberships are **not reimbursable
for any band**, including Band 4 and above. This applies even where the lounge is
bundled into a fare class. Employees who hold personal lounge memberships may use
them but may not claim the cost.

## Seat selection and baggage

- Standard seat selection: reimbursable.
- Extra-legroom or priority seating: not reimbursable below Band 4.
- First checked bag: reimbursable. Additional bags require justification.

## Changes and cancellations

Change fees are reimbursable where the change is driven by business need. Fees
arising from personal preference are not reimbursable.
