# Expense Policy 05 — Spending Limits (Updated)

**Document ID:** expense-05-limits-updated
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Status:** Current. This document supersedes expense-04-limits in full.

## Daily meal limits by band

| Band | Daily meal limit |
| --- | --- |
| Band 1 | EUR 55 |
| Band 2 | EUR 75 |
| Band 3 | EUR 85 |
| Band 4 and above | EUR 110 |

The daily meal limit is a cap on actual spend where per diem is not claimed. An
employee may claim per diem or actual meal costs, not both.

## Hotel nightly caps

| City | Nightly cap |
| --- | --- |
| Berlin | EUR 210 |
| Paris | EUR 250 |
| London | GBP 240 |
| Amsterdam | EUR 220 |
| Mumbai | INR 11000 |
| New York | USD 340 |

Caps are inclusive of local tourist tax and exclusive of breakfast where
breakfast is charged separately.

## Ground transport

Taxi and ride-hailing journeys are reimbursable up to **EUR 60 per journey**.
Above that figure, written justification is required.

Car hire requires advance approval from the line manager. Fuel is reimbursable
against receipts; mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at EUR 0.35 per
kilometre.

## Exceeding a limit

Where a cost exceeds the applicable limit, the employee may claim up to the limit
and must absorb the remainder personally, unless a prior written exception was
granted by the budget holder.

## Transition note

Costs incurred before 1 January 2026 are assessed against expense-04-limits.
Costs incurred on or after that date are assessed against this document.
