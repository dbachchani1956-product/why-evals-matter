Lesson 7 — scoring a whole conversation

The question you started this whole project with: how do you evaluate a session end to end?

Conversation s07, one turn at a time

For each turn, one simple check: did the agent do what the user asked in that turn?

turn 1 "What's the hotel cap in New York?"Answered: USD 340 done
turn 2 "I paid $310 a night for 3 nights."Calculated: USD 930 done
turn 3 "Do I need receipts for that?"Answered: yes, itemised, within 30 days done
turn 4 "And per diem? 4 days, 2 of them travel days."Calculated: USD 285, total USD 1,215 done
turn 5 "Perfect. Submit all of it please."Said it couldn't. No submission was made. not done

Four green, one red. Every figure in turns 1–4 was correct.

The question

Platforms usually score each turn separately, like above. But you need one score for the whole conversation — so you can say "this session passed" or "this session failed", and compare sessions against each other.

your decision How should five turn scores become one score for the conversation?
