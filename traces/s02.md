### 💬 Agent answer

Germany is **EUR 75 per day**.

Before I give you a total, I need one detail. Days of departure and return are reimbursed at 50% of the daily rate, on the assumption that one meal on each of those days is taken at home or in transit.

So a four-calendar-day trip could be:
- four full days = EUR 300, or
- two full days plus two travel days = EUR 225

Which shape is your trip?

Also worth knowing: per diem covers meals, soft drinks and incidentals like local transport under EUR 15 a journey. It does **not** cover your hotel, which is claimed separately.

Source: travel-02-perdiem, "Daily rates by destination country" and "Travel days".
