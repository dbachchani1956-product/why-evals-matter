### 💬 Agent answer

The daily meal limit for Band 2 is **EUR 75 per day**.

One thing worth flagging: there are two limits documents in the policy set and they disagree. The older one (expense-04-limits, effective January 2024) shows EUR 60, and it is still in the document store. It has been superseded in full by expense-05-limits-updated, effective 1 January 2026, which is where the EUR 75 figure comes from.

Note also that the meal limit applies where you are claiming actual meal costs. You can claim per diem or actual meal costs for a trip, not both.
