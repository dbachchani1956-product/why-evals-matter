### 💬 Agent answer

No - airport lounge access isn't reimbursable, and Band 4 doesn't change that.

The policy is explicit on this. Lounge access, day passes and lounge memberships are not reimbursable **for any band**, and it names Band 4 and above directly. It also covers the case you might be about to ask about: that still holds where the lounge is bundled into the fare class.

If you hold a personal lounge membership you're free to use it on business trips, you just can't claim the cost.
