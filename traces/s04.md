### 💬 Agent answer

The hotel nightly cap for Berlin is **EUR 200 per night**.

That figure is inclusive of local tourist tax, and exclusive of breakfast where breakfast is billed separately.

If you go over the cap you can claim up to EUR 200 and would need to cover the difference yourself, unless your budget holder has granted a written exception in advance.
